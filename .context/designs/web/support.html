Asked often enough to answer here.

I bought the unlock and got a new phone.

Open the app, go to the purchase screen and tap Restore. It is tied to your store account, not the device. If it still does not appear, email us with the store receipt.

Why is there no iOS version of my app?

Each app's page says exactly where it stands on each platform, and the Get block shows a disabled button with the reason rather than hiding it.

How do I leave a beta?

On Android, the Play listing has a Leave the programme link. On iOS, delete the TestFlight build. You go back to the public version and keep your purchases.

Do you track me?

Each app's page lists the permissions it asks for and why. Some apps carry no analytics SDK at all — where that is true, the page says so, per app.

Can I suggest a feature or a card pack?

Yes, and it gets read by the person who would build it. Cards and packs in particular come mostly from suggestions.

Where are the privacy policies?

One per app at ritvi-apps.github.io/legal, linked from every app page and from every store listing.
