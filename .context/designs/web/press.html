One line

Ritvi Apps makes small, honest apps in Pune, India — party games and useful tools, with no ads in the games.

Short · ~50 words

Ritvi Apps is an independent studio in Pune, India. It builds party games for people already in the same room, and small utilities for the rest of the week. The games carry no ad SDK at all, purchases are one-time rather than subscriptions, and each app works offline wherever it has no real reason to need a network.

Founder

Ritvi Apps is run by Ritesh Firodiya, a full-stack engineer with around nine years of production experience across Walmart, Swiggy, Globant and Speechify. Every app is built and shipped solo.
