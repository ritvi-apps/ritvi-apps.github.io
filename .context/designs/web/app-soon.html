Why it exists

A photo cannot show the oil a dish was cooked in.

Every camera-first calorie app turns a photo into one confident number anyway. AskCal marks each value Seen, Guessed or Unknown, resolves what it can against USDA data, and asks one or two questions instead of inventing the rest.

  • Seen — visible in the photo and identified.
  • Guessed — inferred from the dish, with the assumption stated.
  • Unknown — it asks, rather than filling in a number.
