The redirect · /go/<slug>

One short link per app that a phone resolves to the right destination — the app's own site, the App Store, or Google Play. This is what goes in a bio, a QR code, a WhatsApp forward or a poster, so the link never has to be reprinted when a platform opens.

It is a page, not a bare meta refresh, for three reasons: a wrong guess must be recoverable, a disabled platform must say why, and a desktop visitor needs something other than a store they cannot install from. The other two destinations always stay visible while it redirects.
